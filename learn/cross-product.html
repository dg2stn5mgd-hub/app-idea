The cross product (vector product)

The cross product takes two vectors and gives you a new vector that is perpendicular to both of them. Its length equals the area of the parallelogram they make.

The formula

a × b = a₁a₂a₃× b₁b₂b₃= a₂b₃ − a₃b₂a₃b₁ − a₁b₃a₁b₂ − a₂b₁

How to remember it: to get a row of the answer, cover that row in a and b and cross-multiply the two rows that are left. The top row gives a₂b₃ − a₃b₂, and the bottom row gives a₁b₂ − a₂b₁. The middle row is where most mistakes happen. It's a₃b₁ − a₁b₃, which is the opposite way round from the pattern, so many students write the cross-multiplication and then flip its sign.

What it means

  • Direction: perpendicular to both a and b, following the right-hand rule. Point your fingers along a and curl them towards b; your thumb points along a × b.
  • Length: |a × b| = |a||b| sin θ, which is the area of the parallelogram with sides a and b.

Key facts to remember

  • a × b is a vector, unlike the dot product.
  • Order matters: b × a = −(a × b).
  • Parallel test: for non-zero vectors, a × b = 0 ⇔ a ∥ b. In particular, a × a = 0.
  • (a × b) · a = 0 and (a × b) · b = 0. Use this to check your answer.
  • Brackets expand normally: a × (b + c) = a × b + a × c. Just keep the order.

Where it shows up in H2

  • Normal to a plane: if a plane contains directions b and c, then n = b × c. This is how you convert r = a + λb + μc into r · n = D.
  • Area of a triangle ABC = ½ |AB × AC|. The area of a parallelogram is |a × b|.
  • Line where two planes meet: its direction is n₁ × n₂.
  • Perpendicular distance from a point to a line: |AP × d̂|. See Projections.
  • Shortest distance between skew lines: project onto d₁ × d₂, the direction perpendicular to both lines.

Worked example

A(1, 0, 2), B(3, 1, 1) and C(2, −1, 4). Find a Cartesian equation of the plane ABC and the area of triangle ABC.

Show solution
  1. AB = (2, 1, −1) and AC = (1, −1, 2)
  2. n = AB × AC = ((1)(2) − (−1)(−1), (−1)(1) − (2)(2), (2)(−1) − (1)(1)) = (1, −5, −3)
  3. Check: n · AB = 2 − 5 + 3 = 0 ✓, and n · AC = 1 + 5 − 6 = 0 ✓
  4. D = n · OA = 1 + 0 − 6 = −5, so the plane is x − 5y − 3z = −5
  5. Area = ½ |n| = ½ √(1 + 25 + 9) = ½√35 ≈ 2.96 units²
Common mistakes
  • Getting the sign of the middle component wrong. Always check (a × b) · a = 0.
  • Swapping the order and not noticing. That's fine for a normal vector, because any multiple works, but not when the direction matters.
  • Forgetting the ½ for the area of a triangle.
  • Mixing up the tests: a · b = 0 means perpendicular, and a × b = 0 means parallel.
